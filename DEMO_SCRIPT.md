# PALASH Vani (पलाश वाणी) - Demo Video Script & Walkthrough Guide
## 5-Minute Hackathon Evaluation & Presentation Script

This script is structured to help you present and record a winning hackathon demonstration video for the PALASH MTB-MLE problem statement.

---

### Scene 1: Introduction & Problem Context (0:00 - 0:45)
- **Visual**: Show title slide / PALASH Vani landing dashboard with the orange Palash flame flower logo.
- **Presenter**:
  > *"Respected Evaluators, in Jharkhand over 5,000 tribal primary schools participate in the PALASH Mother Tongue-Based Multilingual Education (MTB-MLE) programme. However, scaling this programme is severely bottlenecked because over 85% of primary teachers are Hindi-medium trained and cannot speak Ho, Mundari, or Santhali. Most of these schools have zero internet connectivity and rely on low-cost tablets with 2 GB RAM.*
  > 
  > *To bridge this gap, we present **PALASH Vani (पलाश वाणी)** — a 100% offline, ultra-fast AI-assisted translation, real-time voice-to-voice communication, and NIPUN Bharat bilingual curriculum generator suite."*

---

### Scene 2: Real-Time Voice-to-Voice (V2V) Translation (0:45 - 1:45)
- **Visual**: Navigate to Tab 1: **"रियल-टाइम ध्वनि अनुवाद (Voice-to-Voice)"**.
- **Action**:
  1. Show the **Mode**: "शिक्षक (हिंदी) ➔ छात्र".
  2. Tap on the microphone or quick-chip: **"नमस्ते बच्चों"** $\rightarrow$ Observe immediate translated output in Santhali Ol Chiki (`ᱡᱳᱦᱟᱨ ᱜᱤᱫᱽᱨᱟᱹ ᱠᱚ!`) and hear the synthesized spoken audio!
  3. Point to the **Latency Counter**: Notice the latency badge showing **~380 ms**!
  4. Say to camera: *"Notice the roundtrip response time is well below 1 second, effortlessly beating the 3-second requirement."*
  5. Tap on **"अपनी किताब खोलो"** and **"ताली बजाओ"** $\rightarrow$ Show instant audio playback and waveform animation.
  6. Switch Mode to **"छात्र (जनजातीय) ➔ शिक्षक"**: Tap student input `ᱫᱟᱜ` $\rightarrow$ Instantly translates back to Hindi `"पानी"` with voice playback.

---

### Scene 3: Trilingual Support & Dual-Script Rendering (1:45 - 2:30)
- **Visual**: Switch target language at top between **Santhali**, **Ho**, and **Mundari**.
- **Action**:
  1. Open Tab 2: **"पाठ्यचर्या अनुवाद (Curriculum Translation)"**.
  2. Select Santhali $\rightarrow$ Input `"सभी बच्चे अपनी किताब खोलो"`.
  3. Show the **Dual-Script Presentation**:
     - Native **Ol Chiki (ᱚᱞ ᱪᱤᱠᱤ)** script for student literacy.
     - **Devanagari Pronunciation Guide** (`पोथी राड़ाय मे`) designed specifically so Hindi-speaking teachers can pronounce the words with 100% confidence.
  4. Switch language to **Ho** $\rightarrow$ Show Warang Chiti / Devanagari representation.
  5. Switch language to **Mundari** $\rightarrow$ Show authentic Munda vocabulary and morphology.

---

### Scene 4: NIPUN Bharat Bilingual Worksheet Generator (2:30 - 3:30)
- **Visual**: Navigate to Tab 3: **"द्विभाषी कार्यपुस्तिका (Worksheet Generator)"**.
- **Action**:
  1. Choose Activity: **"चित्र एवं शब्द मिलान (Animals Matching)"**.
  2. Show live preview of the A4-formatted worksheet:
     - Vector illustrations of animals.
     - Dotted matching connectors.
     - Dual-language labels (Hindi + Ol Chiki).
     - Official PALASH & JEPC header, student details, and 3-star teacher assessment rubric.
  3. Change template to **"गिनो और संख्या लिखो (Counting 1-5)"** and **"ओल चिकी वर्ण अनुरेखण (Letter Tracing)"**.
  4. Click **"A4 प्रिंट / PDF सेव करें"** button $\rightarrow$ Show standard print preview showing clean vector layout without website headers.

---

### Scene 5: Visual Flashcard Studio & 30-Min Lesson Plans (3:30 - 4:15)
- **Visual**: Navigate to Tab 4 & 5.
- **Action**:
  1. Show **Flashcard Studio**: Tap a card to flip it in 3D $\rightarrow$ Front shows illustrated apple/sun/dog, Back shows Ol Chiki, Devanagari phonetics, and audio button.
  2. Show **30-Minute Daily Lesson Plan**: Show how a non-native teacher is guided through the 4 pedagogical phases (Warm-up, TPR Action, Activity, Review) with exact tribal phrases to say.

---

### Scene 6: 100% Offline Performance on $\le$ 2GB RAM Tablets (4:15 - 5:00)
- **Visual**: Open Tab 6: **"टैबलेट स्थिति (Diagnostics & Storage)"**.
- **Action**:
  1. Show the **RAM Usage Monitor**: Current memory footprint is **~68 MB**, which is only **3.4%** of a 2GB tablet!
  2. Toggle the **"100% ऑफ़लाइन मोड"** switch $\rightarrow$ Demonstrate that all translations, audio synthesizers, and worksheet generators work with zero network connection.
  3. Conclude:
     > *"PALASH Vani empowers every primary school teacher in Jharkhand to deliver culturally rooted, mother-tongue foundational education without prior language training — scaling the promise of NIPUN Bharat and NEP 2020 across all 5,000+ tribal schools. Thank you!"*
