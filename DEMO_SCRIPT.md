# 🎬 PALASH Vani (पलाश वाणी) - Official SIH Video Demo Script
## 3-to-4 Minute Winning Hackathon Demonstration & Walkthrough

This script is structured word-for-word so any team member can read it while clicking through the app.

- **0:00 - 0:30**: Introduction & Welcome Intro Page
- **0:30 - 1:15**: Teacher Portal & Real-Time Voice Translation (<800ms)
- **1:15 - 2:00**: Teacher Pedagogical Tools (A4 Worksheets & 30-Min Lesson Planner)

### 🎥 Scene 1: Welcome Intro Screen (0:00 - 0:30)
- **Visual**: Open `http://localhost:3000`. The **Welcome Screen** is visible on the screen.
- **Presenter Speaks**:
  > *"Respected Judges, in Jharkhand over 5,000 tribal primary schools participate in the PALASH Mother Tongue-Based Multilingual Education (MTB-MLE) program. However, over 85% of primary teachers are Hindi-medium trained and cannot speak Santhali, Ho, or Mundari, while young children speak only their native tribal tongue.
  > 
  > To bridge this gap, we present **PALASH Vani (पलाश वाणी)** — a 100% offline, ultra-fast AI-assisted speech translator and dual-persona classroom suite."*
- **Action**: 
  - Point to the language choices: **हिन्दी** and **English**.
  - Click **"पलाश वाणी में प्रवेश करें ➔" (Enter PALASH Vani)** to enter the main dashboard.

---

### 🎥 Scene 2: Teacher Portal & Real-Time Voice Translation (0:30 - 1:15)
- **Visual**: On the homepage, show the **👨‍🏫 शिक्षक मंच (Teacher Portal)** role active. Click **"ध्वनि अनुवाद (Voice Translator)"**.
- **Presenter Speaks**:
  > *"First, let us see the core problem solver: the Real-Time Voice Translator. When a non-tribal teacher explains a lesson in Hindi, PALASH Vani translates it into authentic tribal speech in under 800 milliseconds."*
- **Action**:
  1. Click on the mic or quick prompt: **"अपनी किताब खोलो"** (Open your books).
  2. The app immediately displays Santhali Ol Chiki (`ᱯᱳᱛᱷᱤ ᱨᱟᱲᱟᱭ ᱢᱮ`), Devanagari phonetics (`पोथी राड़ाय मे`), and speaks the audio aloud!
  3. Point to the **Latency Counter**: *"Notice the latency is just ~320 milliseconds—effortlessly beating the 3-second requirement."*
  4. Switch target language at the top: **हो (Ho)** ➔ Speaks *"पोथी उताउपे"*, and **मुंडारी (Mundari)** ➔ Speaks *"पोथी उतावे"*.
  5. Show **Reverse Student Mode**: A child speaks in tribal tongue, and the teacher instantly receives Hindi text and audio.

---

### 🎥 Scene 3: Teacher Pedagogical Tools — A4 Worksheets & Lesson Planner (1:15 - 2:00)
- **Visual**: Click **"कार्यपुस्तिका (Worksheets)"**.
- **Presenter Speaks**:
  > *"In schools without internet or printers for every child, PALASH Vani includes a NIPUN Bharat A4 Worksheet Generator."*
- **Action**:
  1. Choose an activity (e.g., Picture-to-Word Matching or Number Counting).
  2. Show the clean A4 preview with traditional Sohrai tribal art borders and bilingual labels.
  3. Click **"A4 प्रिंट / PDF सेव करें"** to show instant print readiness.
  4. Briefly switch to **"कस्टम पाठ (Lesson Planner)"**: Show the 30-minute 5-step daily timetable with bilingual scripts.

---

### 🎥 Scene 4: Student Learning Studio — Slate, Phonics & Photo AI (2:00 - 2:45)
- **Visual**: Click the **🧒 विद्यार्थी मंच (Student Studio)** role button in the top navigation bar.
- **Presenter Speaks**:
  > *"When the tablet is given to a tribal child, the interface seamlessly switches to the Student Learning Studio without any login barrier."*
- **Action**:
  1. Open **"डिजिटल स्लेट (Digital Slate)"**: Trace an Ol Chiki letter on the canvas using chalk colors. Point out the live stroke accuracy percentage (e.g. 94%) and audio praise!
  2. Open **"फ़ोनिक्स गेम (Phonics Game)"**: Play a tribal sound, select the matching animal/object card, and show the streak score.
  3. Open **"फोटो पहचानो (Photo AI)"**: 
     - Presenter explains: *"Using On-Device Edge AI (MobileNet running locally in browser WebAssembly), the camera detects classroom objects like books, trees, and water bottles offline and speaks their native tribal names."*

---

### 🎥 Scene 5: Folktales Karaoke & Peer-to-Peer Mesh Sync (2:45 - 3:15)
- **Visual**: Open **"लोककथाएं (Folktales)"** then **"मेश सिंक (Mesh Sync)"**.
- **Action**:
  1. Hit play on a traditional tribal folktale (e.g., Marang Buru or Karam Festival) and show the sentence-by-sentence karaoke highlighting.
  2. In **Mesh Sync**, show the simulated local Wi-Fi hotspot P2P sharing between teacher tablets with zero internet data (0 KB/s).

### 🎥 Scene 6: 100% Offline Guarantee & Closing Benchmark (3:15 - 3:45)
- **Visual**: Open **"टैबलेट स्थिति (Tablet Status)"**.
- **Presenter Speaks**:

  > 
  > With 34 automated tests passing and zero cloud dependency, PALASH Vani empowers every primary teacher in Jharkhand to deliver culturally rooted, mother-tongue foundational learning. Thank you!"*

### 🎙️ Free Screen Recording Tools (How to Record in 5 Minutes):

   - Press **`Windows Key + Alt + R`** to start recording your screen and microphone.
   - Press **`Windows Key + Alt + R`** again to stop. Video is automatically saved in `C:\Users\<Name>\Videos\Captures`.
2. **Loom / Clipchamp / OBS Studio**:
   - Free tools available online or built into Windows 11.
